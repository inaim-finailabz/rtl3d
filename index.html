RTL@3D Beirut 1.5.2

A 3D desktop for Ubuntu and Kubuntu, in the spirit of Sun's Looking Glass. Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later).

Install

curl -fsSL https://inaim-finailabz.github.io/rtl3d/get | bash

Then log out and choose RTL@3D on the login screen. Updates come with your system's own updates (sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade, or Software Updater / Discover).

Suites here: questing resolute. Packages for amd64 and arm64.
